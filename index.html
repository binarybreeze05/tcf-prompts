TCF Canada — Prompts

Every expression écrite topic (Tasks 1 · 2 · 3) and expression orale topic (Tasks 2 · 3) published by reussir-tcfcanada.com between January 2022 and August 2026, deduplicated and ranked by practice value.

Default order: similarity. Each prompt sits next to its nearest lexical neighbour. Open anywhere and the items around you rehearse the same vocabulary.
Coverage order — one click. Rank 1 = the prompt whose mastery opens the most other prompts. Each item carries the why of its rank, anchored on the TCF rubric (Linguistique · Pragmatique · Sociolinguistique) with a September-exam NCLC 7 target.
Difficulty order — rank #1 = easiest. Useful for a warm-up ramp instead of hitting abstract topics cold.
Marking your progress — every prompt carries a Done tick. The counter at the top of each page tracks what is left, filters finished items out of any chosen order, and remembers your ticks between visits. Ticks are stored per pool, so a prompt ticked off on the English page is already ticked on the French one.
